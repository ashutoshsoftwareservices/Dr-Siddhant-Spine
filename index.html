Professional Biography

Dr. Siddhant Navik
Consultant Spine Surgeon • Senior Resident
MBBS, D. Ortho | FISS (Spine Surgery) • FMISS (MIS Spine Surgery)

Dr. Siddhant Navik is an accomplished Consultant Spine Surgeon currently serving as a Senior Resident at ESIS Hospital, Vashi. With over 8+ years of dedicated clinical and surgical expertise (2018 to Present), he has built a stellar medical foundation through key positions at premier institutions including Wockhardt Hospital (Mumbai Central), ESIS MGM Hospital, Reliance Hospital, and Mumbai Port Trust.

As a dual-fellowship trained specialist in Spine Surgery (FISS) and Minimally Invasive Spine Surgery (FMISS), Dr. Siddhant focuses on precision-guided, patient-centric interventions. He specializes in advanced cervical spine decompression for complex neck pain, cervical radiculopathy, and cervical myelopathy—performing procedures such as cervical laminectomy and Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF). His clinical expertise also encompasses keyhole discectomies, tubular decompressions, endoscopic spine surgery, and complex lumbar and cervical spinal fusions. Guided by a dedication to evidence-based medicine, he prioritizes minimal tissue trauma and rapid post-operative recovery.

Key Surgical Experience

Served as an Assistant Surgeon at Wockhardt Hospital, Mumbai Central, acquiring hands-on surgical exposure in Upper & Lower Limb Trauma Management, Arthroplasty (Total Knee & Total Hip Replacement), and Arthroscopic Surgeries (ACL Reconstruction & Rotator Cuff Repair).

Spine Care Philosophy

"My mission as a spine surgeon is to eliminate debilitating back and neck pain while preserving natural spinal biomechanics. By using state-of-the-art minimally invasive techniques, we minimize surgical trauma and return patients to an active, pain-free life swiftly."

Specialized Cervical Session Cervical Spine (C1–C7 Region)

Cervical Spine Care (Neck & Nerve Conditions)

Super-specialized clinical evaluation and minimally invasive surgical management for disorders affecting the neck vertebrae (C1–C7), nerve roots, and the cervical spinal cord. Dr. Siddhant Navik provides evidence-based treatments designed to restore mobility and protect neurological health.

ACDF (Anterior Cervical Fusion) Cervical Micro-Decompression Endoscopic Cervical Discectomy
Neck Pain (Cervicalgia)

Acute & chronic neck strain, muscle spasms, cervical disc degeneration, facet joint arthritis, and neck stiffness.

Cervical Radiculopathy

Pinched cervical nerve root causing shooting pain, numbness, tingling, and radiating discomfort into shoulders, arms, and fingers.

Cervical Myelopathy

Spinal cord compression resulting in hand weakness, fine-motor clumsiness (buttoning shirt), gait instability & balance issues.
